# Cross-domain prior art for theological investigation

**Date:** 2026-08-09  
**Question:** What mature practices from evidence synthesis, law, intelligence analysis, safety assurance, provenance, and adjacent domains should Corvus borrow for Question-proportionate investigation, Claim-relative source authority, competing Positions, evidence lineage, reproducibility, Material saturation, adversarial verification, calibrated conclusions, privacy, and auditable presentation?

## Recommendation

Use **systematic and living evidence synthesis as the lead model for the Investigation control plane**, then borrow narrower controls from four complementary domains:

1. **Cochrane/PRISMA for the research protocol:** derive explicit Answer obligations, eligibility rules, source lanes, search and update policy, exclusions, and a reproducible stopping record from the question before synthesis.
2. **Safety/assurance cases for the package:** preserve Corvus's existing claim/context/derivation/evidence structure, including visibly undeveloped obligations and a defined scope of application.
3. **Legal research for authority and currentness:** evaluate authority inside a jurisdiction-like namespace, distinguish primary authority from finding aids, and run a mandatory subsequent-treatment/currentness check on every authority-bearing source.
4. **Structured intelligence analysis for challenge:** compare evidence across serious alternative Positions, separate source quality from the credibility of a particular assertion, and explicitly state what information gap or assumption limits a conclusion.
5. **W3C PROV and nanopublications for lineage semantics:** keep the proposition, the provenance of that proposition, and the provenance of the package that published it distinct.

This is a **hybrid, not a transplant**. The best lead is evidence synthesis because it covers the whole investigation lifecycle—question framing, pre-specified selection, bias-aware search, structured findings, transparent reporting, and updates. Assurance cases fit Corvus's output graph better, but they do not by themselves tell an investigator how broadly to search, which competing bodies of material to include, or when an inquiry is current enough to stop.

Do **not** import medical evidence hierarchies, legal binding-precedent semantics, intelligence reliability numbers, safety-case claims of proof, RDF infrastructure, or probabilistic confidence language into theological judgments. Corvus's existing Tradition-relative authority, Claim-relative source authority, categorical Answer outcome, scoped Evidence gaps, and prohibition on numeric theological confidence are the correct domain boundary.

## Method and epistemic labels

This review used first-party standards, handbooks, manuals, and specifications owned by the relevant institutions or standards bodies. It is design prior art, not evidence that the borrowed methods have already been validated for theology.

- **Source fact** reports what a cited owner document prescribes or defines.
- **Corvus inference** is the proposed translation into the Theological assistant. These inferences are recommendations, not claims made by the cited source.

## Ranked findings

### 1. Evidence synthesis is the strongest lead

#### Source facts

Cochrane begins with the review question and scope, pre-specified inclusion criteria, searching and selection, data collection, bias assessment, synthesis, certainty assessment, reporting, and updating as distinct parts of one review method. Its search guidance calls for a thorough, objective, reproducible search across a range of sources **within resource limits**, warns that one database is not adequate, and treats studies rather than their multiple reports as the unit of interest. Search strategies should be peer reviewed, and selection follows pre-specified eligibility criteria. [Cochrane Handbook, Chapter 2](https://training.cochrane.org/handbook/current/chapter-02) · [Chapter 4](https://training.cochrane.org/handbook/current/chapter-04)

Cochrane separately assesses risk of bias and the certainty of the body of evidence for each outcome. Its GRADE presentation requires the reasons for certainty judgments to be documented; the relevant domains include risk of bias, inconsistency, indirectness, imprecision, and publication bias. A structured “Summary of findings” makes the principal results and their limitations inspectable. [Cochrane Handbook, Chapter 7](https://training.cochrane.org/handbook/current/chapter-07) · [Chapter 14](https://training.cochrane.org/handbook/current/chapter-14)

PRISMA 2020 is a **reporting** standard, not a guarantee of review quality. Its checklist calls for the question and eligibility criteria; every information source and last search date; full search strategies; selection and collection processes; excluded near-miss studies and reasons; methods for bias, synthesis, heterogeneity, sensitivity, and certainty assessment; results of search and selection; limitations; registration and protocol; and data/code availability. [PRISMA 2020 statement and checklist](https://www.prisma-statement.org/prisma-2020) · [expanded checklist](https://www.prisma-statement.org/s/PRISMA_2020_expanded_checklist-yc78.pdf)

Cochrane does not make every review “living.” It says an update decision should depend on the continuing importance of the question and whether new data or methods could meaningfully affect the findings. Living reviews add pre-specified search frequency, incorporation triggers, status communication, and criteria for leaving living mode; new evidence is incorporated when it is likely to affect conclusions or on a declared fixed schedule. [Cochrane Handbook, Chapter IV](https://training.cochrane.org/handbook/current/chapter-iv) · [Cochrane living-systematic-review guidance](https://resources.cochrane.org/sites/default/files/uploads/inline-files/Transform/201912_LSR_Revised_Guidance.pdf)

#### Corvus inference

The useful abstraction is not “do a medical systematic review for every question.” It is **protocol before synthesis, proportional execution after classification**:

- Preserve the question as written, resolve explicit Analysis scope, and derive Answer obligations before retrieval.
- Select a versioned investigation profile from observable question features: factual versus interpretive, scoped versus unscoped, contested versus undisputed, authority-sensitive, original-language-sensitive, historically time-bounded, or currentness-sensitive.
- For that profile, declare the required source lanes, Position plan, eligibility/admission rules, challenge operations, update check, and stopping rule.
- Treat a conceptual work and the artifacts that report or deliver it separately, just as Cochrane distinguishes a study from its reports. This reinforces Corvus's work/edition/artifact/Citation identity rather than letting duplicate webpages masquerade as independent support.
- Record searched lanes, admitted and excluded near-misses with reason codes, last-checked dates, and scope changes. Present only Material operations in the Answer evidence package; keep unused exploratory traffic out of the user-visible graph.
- Use a compact summary of obligations, Positions, evidence state, and gaps as the theological analogue of a Summary of findings—not GRADE's evidence hierarchy or certainty score.

This gives Question-proportionate investigation a reproducible meaning. A simple, explicitly scoped textual question may use one exact primary passage and one validation pass. A contested question expands to the benchmark-required Position memberships and their authority-bearing primary sources. A currentness-sensitive or corpus-gap question adds Live research source admission and a source-update check. Users never choose a “deep research mode”; the plan expands because the obligations do.

### 2. Assurance cases are the strongest package-level complement

#### Source facts

The UK Ministry of Defence defines a safety case as a structured argument, supported by a body of evidence, that makes a compelling, comprehensible, and valid case that a system is safe **for a given application in a given environment**. Defence Standard 00-056 requires the evidence to come from the defined information set and the case to address safety over the relevant lifecycle. [UK MOD, Defence Standard 00-056 Part 1](https://www.contractsfinder.service.gov.uk/Notice/Attachment/02f13f3e-57ae-4b0d-88e0-b20cc7dbe81b) · [MOD explanation of an Air System Safety Case](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/implementation-of-improved-air-system-safety-case-regulation-ra-1205)

The owner of the Goal Structuring Notation, the Safety-Critical Systems Club Assurance Case Working Group, describes GSN as an authoritative notation and best-practice guide for argument owners, readers, authors, and approvers. The notation explicitly relates claims, strategies, context, assumptions/justifications, subclaims, and evidence; it can also mark an argument element as undeveloped. [SCSC, GSN Standard](https://scsc.uk/gsn-standard) · [GSN Community Standard v1 hosted by the FAA](https://www.faa.gov/about/office_org/headquarters_offices/ang/redac/redac-sas-201503-gsn-community-standard-v1.pdf)

#### Corvus inference

Corvus already embodies the best parts of an assurance case:

| Assurance-case concept | Corvus concept |
| --- | --- |
| Top claim/subclaim | Answer obligation and atomic Answer statement |
| Defined application/environment | Analysis scope and Position membership scope |
| Argument strategy | typed `derivedFrom` lineage and semantic structure links |
| Solution/evidence | Citation plus statement-owned Evidence link |
| Context/assumption/justification | requested/default/unresolved scope and explicit qualifiers |
| Undeveloped goal | scoped Evidence gap and unsupported obligation |
| Information set | pinned Corpus snapshot plus admitted Live research sources |
| Case snapshot | immutable Answer evidence package with release pins |

The addition worth borrowing is an explicit **assurance boundary** on every conclusion: the scope and complete set of upstream obligations for which the conclusion is valid. A conclusion that depends on only two Position memberships must not visually imply that it resolves all Christianity. An unsupported subclaim remains visibly undeveloped rather than disappearing from a polished answer.

What not to import: a diagram does not make an argument sound, and a theological answer is not a proof that a doctrine is universally true. Corvus should validate structural completeness and evidence authorization, not claim metaphysical certainty. It also should not adopt GSN as another serialization; ADR 0005's typed package is already the deeper domain-specific contract.

### 3. Law supplies the best authority-and-currentness control

#### Source facts

The Law Library of Congress distinguishes governmental primary sources—laws, orders, decisions, and regulations—from secondary sources that explain and help locate primary law but lack controlling or binding authority. [Law Library of Congress, Legal Research: Secondary Resources](https://guides.loc.gov/law-secondary-resources)

Legal authority is contextual rather than a publisher popularity score. The Federal Judicial Center's judicial-writing manual advises supporting settled propositions with controlling circuit authority and using other circuits when no in-circuit authority exists; secondary sources are not primary authority. [Federal Judicial Center, *Judicial Writing Manual*](https://www.nhd.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/pdf/HR/Judicial%20Writing%20Manual.pdf)

Citators add a distinct validation step after discovery. The Library of Congress describes citators as tracking subsequent decisions that cited a case or laws affecting a statute, including whether a case was questioned, overturned, or affirmed. California Courts' Shepard's guide distinguishes negative, questioned, cautionary, and positive subsequent treatment and lets the researcher inspect the exact later passage. [Library of Congress, cataloging manual §34.3.2](https://www.loc.gov/aba/pcc/conser/CCM/Module34.pdf) · [California Courts, *Using Shepard's Online*](https://www.courts.ca.gov/documents/Using-Shepards-Online.pdf)

#### Corvus inference

Borrow three controls:

1. **Namespace authority.** A source's authority is evaluated under a named recognizing body, jurisdiction-like scope, source kind, and claim type. This strongly supports ADR 0002 and the newer Claim-relative source authority rule; it does not create a cross-tradition hierarchy.
2. **Finding aid versus authority.** Search results, embeddings, Gloo/model output, reference works, and scholarship can discover or contextualize. They do not inherit the authority of the primary artifact they point toward.
3. **Subsequent-treatment validation.** Admission should ask whether the exact edition or statement was corrected, superseded, rescinded, retranslated, officially clarified, or received differently by the body whose position it is being used to establish. A stale authority creates a scoped Evidence gap or requires a newer Citation; it is not silently retained because its URL still resolves.

Do not import “binding precedent.” Christian traditions do not share one appellate hierarchy, and later reception is not reducible to red/green citator flags. Corvus should store typed treatment assertions with exact later sources and recognizing-body scope, then apply deterministic rules only where that body has supplied a determinate status.

### 4. Intelligence analysis supplies the best adversarial check

#### Source facts

ODNI's ICD 203 requires analytic products to describe source quality and credibility, express and explain uncertainty, distinguish underlying information from assumptions and judgments, incorporate analysis of alternatives, and base judgments on all available sources. It says uncertainty explanations should identify causes such as information type, currency, amount, knowledge gaps, and assumptions, and, where appropriate, indicators that would change the judgment. [ODNI, ICD 203 Analytic Standards](https://www.dni.gov/files/documents/ICD/ICD-203.pdf)

The CIA's structured-tradecraft primer recommends Analysis of Competing Hypotheses, Devil's Advocacy, and Team A/Team B for different failure modes. ACH compares evidence against multiple hypotheses and emphasizes evidence that discriminates among them; Devil's Advocacy challenges a dominant view, while Team A/Team B clarifies two strongly held competing views. [CIA, *A Tradecraft Primer: Structured Analytic Techniques for Improving Intelligence Analysis*](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/955180a45afe3f5013772c313b16face/Tradecraft-Primer-apr09.pdf)

NATO doctrine evaluates source reliability and information credibility independently so that a judgment about the collection capability does not leak into the rating of the particular information, or vice versa. [NATO AJP-2.1, §3.36 and Table 3.1](https://coi.nato.int/EWCOI/C2%20of%20EW%20Library/CRB%20Development%20Working%20Folder/Doctrine%20Concepts%20%28DOC%29/AJP-2.1%20EDB%20V1%20E.pdf)

#### Corvus inference

Add one bounded **challenge pass** after the candidate package is assembled but before the deterministic evidence gate:

- Construct the benchmark-required competing Positions before judging which evidence is persuasive; do not build alternatives only after a favored answer has formed.
- For every conclusion, identify the smallest upstream statement whose removal or reversal would change it.
- Search specifically for admitted evidence that challenges, qualifies, or makes that statement non-diagnostic between Positions.
- Keep source authenticity/authority separate from whether the cited passage supports the complete claim. An impeccable official source can be irrelevant or misquoted; a relevant secondary analysis cannot independently establish an official Tradition position.
- Emit only Material challenge results: a challenging/qualifying Citation, a changed statement or Position, or an Evidence gap. Do not expose brainstorming, private chain-of-thought, or rejected hypothetical reasoning.

Do not import source-reliability letters, likelihood percentages, classified-source handling, or a single confidence number. The useful control is separation of dimensions and deliberate disconfirmation, not the intelligence scales.

### 5. PROV and nanopublications sharpen lineage without replacing Corvus's schema

#### Source facts

W3C PROV models provenance using entities, activities, and agents, with relations including usage, generation, derivation, attribution, and association. It defines provenance as a record of the people, institutions, entities, and activities involved in producing or influencing a thing, and provides constraints for validating provenance records. [W3C, PROV-DM Recommendation](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-dm/) · [PROV overview](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-overview/)

The nanopublication guidelines separate three graphs: an assertion, provenance describing how that assertion came to be, and publication information describing the nanopublication itself. This prevents authorship or publication metadata from being mistaken for evidence for the assertion. [Nanopublication Guidelines](https://nanopub.net/guidelines/working_draft/)

#### Corvus inference

Use these as a semantic audit checklist:

| Provenance concept | Corvus mapping |
| --- | --- |
| Assertion | atomic Answer statement |
| Assertion provenance | Evidence link or `derivedFrom` lineage |
| Publication provenance | package, schema, model/provider, release, and operation identities |
| Entity | exact source artifact, Citation, input evidence bundle, or package |
| Activity | Material operation |
| Agent/association | responsible provider/module and its role |
| Usage/generation | Operation input and output links |
| Derivation | typed Answer-statement lineage |

The important test is: **can the system distinguish “the source says X,” “Corvus derived Y from X,” and “provider/model Z produced this package version”?** If those collapse into one `source` field, provenance has failed.

Do not add RDF, SPARQL, global reusable Claims, or generic PROV records to the Product runtime. Corvus's closed TypeScript union and deterministic cross-reference validator are more appropriate. PROV is a conformance lens, not a storage decision.

### 6. IPCC calibration and privacy are useful guardrails, not core models

#### Source facts

The IPCC evaluates findings through the type, amount, quality, and consistency of evidence and the degree of agreement, and emphasizes traceability from a headline finding to the underlying assessment. It separates qualitative confidence from probabilistic likelihood, noting that multiple consistent independent lines of high-quality evidence are more robust. [IPCC AR6 Working Group I, Chapter 1, Box 1.1](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/chapter/chapter-1/)

The NIST Privacy Framework calls for inventorying processing purposes, data actions, data elements, actors, and processing environments; establishing use and retention policies; minimizing data; maintaining provenance and lineage; and making processing visible. [NIST Privacy Framework v1.0](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/CSWP/NIST.CSWP.01162020.pdf)

#### Corvus inference

Borrow the IPCC's separation of **amount/quality of evidence** from **agreement**, because a well-attested disagreement is not low-quality research. In Corvus, those dimensions should remain categorical package facts: evidence coverage, material Position agreement/disagreement, gaps, and Answer outcome. Do not use the IPCC confidence or probability vocabulary for theological truth.

For privacy, make the reproducibility boundary the self-contained Answer evidence package and short-lived Replay reference—not durable conversation logging. Inventory each data flow, pin its purpose, and retain only source-oriented artifacts and content-free Operational telemetry. Material-operation receipts may include sanitized inputs and version identities needed for audit/replay; they must exclude raw user questions from telemetry, inferred beliefs, provider exchanges, unused exploration, and chain-of-thought. This matches ADRs 0006 and 0009 while making the privacy rationale auditable.

## Operational definition of Material saturation

No source reviewed supplies a universal source-count threshold suitable for theology. Cochrane explicitly balances search thoroughness with resources, warns that scarce evidence warrants caution before stopping, and makes living updates depend on whether new evidence is likely to affect conclusions. Legal citators show why a source can cease to be fit even after it was found. Therefore the following is a **Corvus inference**, not an external standard:

An investigation reaches **Material saturation** only when all of these are true:

1. Every required Answer obligation is supported, or an unresolved Evidence gap precisely names why it cannot be supported.
2. Every benchmark-required materially distinct Position is represented with independently admitted evidence, or has a disclosed membership-scoped Evidence gap.
3. Every source-grounded statement passes identity, authenticity, authority/scope, rights/deliverability, currentness/subsequent-treatment, and whole-claim-support checks.
4. Every Derived statement has complete acyclic lineage to supported source-grounded statements.
5. The bounded challenge pass found no unaddressed admitted evidence that defeats, materially qualifies, or creates another required Position.
6. The last planned search increment produced no new material Position, changed required statement, repaired required gap, or authority/currentness change; its source lane and date are recorded.

The package should carry a derived `researchCompletion` result:

- `materiallySaturated`: the conditions above pass;
- `completeWithScopedGaps`: the planned source lanes are complete but named obligations remain unsupported; or
- `ceilingReached`: a time, provider, rights, membership, or operation budget stopped work before the conditions passed.

This record does not replace `Answered | Partially answered | Abstained`. It explains whether research completion and answer support are aligned. A ceiling must never be relabeled as saturation.

## Small prototype and evaluation

Build a fixture-only **Investigation ledger** beside, not inside, the production contract. The prototype needs only three new candidate records:

1. `InvestigationPlan`: question classification, obligations, required Positions, source lanes, admission/currentness rules, challenge steps, and stop/update rule.
2. `ChallengeReceipt`: challenged statement/Position, technique (`alternativeComparison`, `devilsAdvocacy`, or `subsequentTreatment`), and only material outputs.
3. `SaturationRecord`: condition results, last search increment, unsearched lanes, completion status, and ceiling reason.

Run six deterministic fixtures:

| Fixture | Expected proportional behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Matthew 22:37–40 factual question | exact Scripture evidence, no unnecessary tradition search, saturated |
| Explicitly Catholic catechetical question | Catholic authority lane plus consequential-disagreement check, no inferred broader lens |
| Baptism salvation/infant-baptism question | full accepted benchmark Position plan, alternatives challenge, no premature saturation |
| Same contested question with one required primary source missing | scoped membership gap and downgraded outcome |
| Authority source later superseded by an official document | subsequent-treatment check replaces or qualifies it |
| Provider ceiling before one Position lane completes | `ceilingReached`, explicit incomplete-research disclosure, never saturated |

Compare the current fixture path with the ledger-enabled path on:

- obligation and material-Position recall against the accepted benchmark;
- exact-claim citation coverage and source-scope correctness;
- detection of seeded contradictory, qualifying, superseding, and duplicate-report evidence;
- correct `Answered | Partially answered | Abstained` derivation;
- correct separation of saturation, scoped gaps, and operational ceilings;
- deterministic reproduction from the same release pins and fixture providers;
- absence of questions, answers, beliefs, raw evidence, and provider content from telemetry; and
- reader ability to identify the source, scope, challenge, and limiting gap for a conclusion in under one minute.

The prototype is successful only if it catches seeded stale/contrary evidence and prevents premature completion **without** expanding the simple fixture into a multi-tradition research workflow. That is the falsifiable test of Question-proportionate investigation.

## Decision implications for Corvus

The research supports the current ADR direction and suggests a focused addition rather than an architectural reset:

- Keep ADR 0005's Answer evidence package, typed statements, evidence roles, categorical outcomes, and graph derivation.
- Keep ADRs 0002–0004's tradition-namespaced source authority and exact artifact model.
- Keep ADR 0006's one explicit, stateless Investigation module; place planning, challenge, and saturation in its visible top-level path.
- Keep ADR 0008's hard boundary between Discovery candidates and admissible evidence.
- Add a versioned Investigation-plan contract, source-currentness/subsequent-treatment validation, one bounded material challenge pass, and a research-completion record.
- Treat search logs and alternative brainstorming as internal transient work. Only plan facts and Material operations that affected the answer or exposed a gap belong in the audit package.

The most important borrowed rule is simple: **a transparent answer is the product of a pre-declared, challengeable investigation, not merely prose with citations attached afterward.**
